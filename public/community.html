Community and Moderation Rules

Effective September 26, 2026. CourseMates connects adults for study conversations. Treat peers as people with boundaries.

Allowed and expected

Discuss study topics, compare approaches, ask questions, and disagree respectfully. Get consent before sharing personal material. Leave any conversation that feels uncomfortable. Remember that peers are anonymous and are not verified students.

Prohibited conduct

  • Harassment, hate, threats, intimidation, or encouragement of self-harm.
  • Sexual exploitation, sexual content involving minors, non-consensual intimate content, or unwanted sexual advances.
  • Sharing personal information about others without permission, impersonation, stalking, or coercion.
  • Scams, malware, spam, repeated unsolicited promotion, or attempts to exploit or overload the service.
  • Copyright infringement, unlawful material, or conduct that violates applicable law.
  • Bypassing bans or deliberately defeating safety controls.

Report and block

Use the chat report control to select the relevant category. Blocking ends the interaction and prevents matching with that browser identifier while the block is retained. Reports contain category, time, and pseudonymous identifiers; the application does not preserve a copy of the conversation for moderators. A report is a request for review, not a guarantee of action.

How enforcement works

Automated text checks may reject abusive text before delivery. Operators can review report metadata, close reports, and impose or lift browser bans. Decisions consider severity and repeated reports; report counts alone are not proof of abuse. Mistakes are possible. Anonymous browser bans do not reliably identify a person and may be bypassed by changing or clearing browsers. Do not rely on filtering or bans as a guarantee of safety.

Appeals and urgent concerns

For moderation appeals and safety concerns, contact the operator contact is not configured in this local development instance. Include a report reference and approximate time if available. Do not send illegal imagery. Reports are not monitored as an emergency service; contact local emergency services for immediate danger.
