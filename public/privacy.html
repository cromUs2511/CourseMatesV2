Privacy Policy

Effective September 26, 2026. CourseMates is anonymous chat for adults. Anonymous access does not mean your use is invisible to the service, your network, or third-party providers.

What the service processes

The service uses a generated handle and public session identifier, chosen interests, chat text, images, voice clips, reactions, music selections, and connection information to match peers and deliver a conversation. It does not require an email address or verify identity or student status. The server and hosting network necessarily receive your IP address. The application uses connection information temporarily for abuse rate limiting and does not include raw IP addresses in its structured application logs.

Cookies and browser storage

A necessary HttpOnly session cookie restores access for up to eight hours. A separate necessary browser safety cookie supports blocking and moderation. Cookies marked HttpOnly are unavailable to page JavaScript. Appearance, theme, and other interface preferences may be stored locally in your browser. These features are functional; the application does not include advertising trackers. Third-party media has separate practices.

Retention and deletion

Live sessions, matchmaking state, chat messages, and media are held in bounded server memory. Chats are not a permanent archive. Messages and their media are removed when deleted, evicted from the room buffer, or when the room ends or expires; a server restart clears live sessions and chats. Sign-out ends your session. The service stores limited safety records separately: pseudonymous browser identifiers, report category and time, the reported user's connection IP, block relationships, moderation action, and expiry. Reports and their IP addresses are retained for up to 30 days. Temporary restrictions expire after their selected period; permanent IP restrictions remain until lifted by an operator. Safety records do not contain chat message text or media. Clearing your browser cookies removes the local identifiers but does not immediately delete unexpired safety records.

Logs, hosting, and backups

Application logs contain operational events, request identifiers, status codes, and timing, not message bodies, email addresses, tokens, or media. Hosting providers and reverse proxies may maintain their own security logs; the deployment operator controls their access and retention. Backups should contain only unexpired moderation metadata, be encrypted, and expire within 30 days. Chats and media are not backed up.

AI providers and YouTube

Using the AI chatbot sends your submitted text and limited recent text context to the configured Gemini or Groq provider. Conversation starters may send selected topic, campus, or discipline context. Do not submit sensitive information to AI features. Provider handling and retention are governed by the operator's provider account and the provider's policies. YouTube search sends the search query to Google through the server. Embedded YouTube playback can send your IP address, device information, and cookies to Google. The service does not promise that deleting a chat deletes provider-held data.

Sharing and security

Selected peers receive the content you send and may keep their own copies. Authorized operators can access limited safety records, including reported IP addresses, for moderation; the operator's infrastructure processes chat in transit. Production traffic uses HTTPS, but messages are not end-to-end encrypted. No internet service can promise absolute security. Do not share contact details, passwords, financial data, or another person's private information.

Your choices and requests

You can leave, delete your own chat messages while available, sign out, clear local preferences and cookies, decline camera or microphone access, and avoid AI or YouTube features. Contact the operator for access, deletion, correction, or privacy questions. Because access is anonymous, the operator may be unable to locate records after browser identifiers are removed and must avoid disclosing another person's information. Applicable privacy rights remain unaffected.
